# Reader Content Priority Map

## Priority 1: answer the scientific question

- Research question and summary
- Complete Knowledge Background and Idea Report
- Research-object rationale, evidence, comparison, and expected result
- Literature depth only when declared in the manifest

## Priority 2: support a decision

- Portfolio role and current decision
- Recommended reading order
- Experimental constraints and success criteria

## Priority 3: inspect provenance

- Detailed evaluation
- Source audits
- Files and downloads
- Publication/build metadata

## Explicitly de-emphasized

PDF count, internal status codes, reviewer audit metadata, scores, kill criteria, provider usage, receipts, and placeholder reports are not first-screen research content.
