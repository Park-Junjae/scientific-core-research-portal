# Product purity as an exposure-time distribution

## Historical hypothesis

Target-only and multi-edit molecules may differ in the exposure time required to accumulate edits. A time-limited active state could therefore change product composition even when static reach is unchanged.

## Evidence boundary

The historical run did not show that a practical TALED control can generate this kinetic separation in mitochondria. The concept is retained as a measurable coordinate, not as an active design recommendation.

## Kill criterion

Drop the hypothesis if shortening exposure reduces target-only and multi-edit products proportionally or if burden dominates the observed product shift.
