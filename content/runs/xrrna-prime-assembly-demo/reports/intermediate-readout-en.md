# Molecular-identity readout

## Why this comes first

A causal hypothesis about an early prime-editing intermediate cannot be evaluated when the assay only measures mature or alternative DNA products. This measurement-first program asks whether target linkage, template provenance, sequence, length class, and recovery bias can be resolved on the same molecule.

## Current boundary

This is a measurement-development direction. It is not an editor hypothesis and it does not imply that the proposed intermediate exists at a useful abundance.

## Decisive experiment

Use known-composition references and blinded mixtures to establish class-specific recovery, false assignment, and uncertainty before interpreting biological samples.

## Kill criterion

Park causal attribution if the required molecular identity cannot be established or if recovery differences can reverse the biological decision.
