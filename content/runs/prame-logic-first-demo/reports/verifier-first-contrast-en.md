# Verifier-first contrast

## Purpose

Define the smallest set of controls and readouts that can separate target-state dependence, broad toxicity, and selection artifacts before a transfer claim is evaluated.

## Required contrasts

The demonstration uses state-positive and state-negative contexts, an inactive intervention control, and a burden-matched control. The analysis reports ambiguity instead of forcing a positive or negative result when these contrasts disagree.

## Stop rule

Do not proceed if the primary readout cannot distinguish the intended causal route from broad burden.
